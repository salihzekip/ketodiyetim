What to Eat and Avoid on the Keto Diet

5 min read · Food Guide · August 12, 2026

The most common question about the keto diet is: "What am I going to eat?" The answer is actually quite broad — only carb sources are removed, and almost everything else is allowed.

✅ Foods You Can Eat Freely

  • Meat: Beef, lamb, chicken (with skin), turkey, bacon, sausage (unsweetened)
  • Fish & Seafood: Salmon, mackerel, tuna, sea bass, shrimp
  • Eggs: Cooked any way you like
  • Leafy Greens: Arugula, spinach, lettuce, parsley, dill
  • Cruciferous & Squash Veggies: Zucchini, broccoli, cauliflower, eggplant, peppers (in moderation)
  • Fats: Olive oil, butter, coconut oil, avocado oil
  • Nuts: Almonds, walnuts, hazelnuts (in small amounts)

❌ Strictly Forbidden

  • Bread, rice, pasta, bulgur, all grains
  • Sugar, honey, molasses, all sweets
  • Fruit juice, soda, alcohol (beer especially)
  • Potatoes, corn, peas, carrots (high starch)
  • Ready-made sauces, ketchup, packaged snacks

⚠️ Consume with Caution

  • Legumes: once a week, small amounts (lentils, chickpeas)
  • Tomatoes: limit to half a tomato per day
  • Onion, garlic: contain a small amount of carbs, use in moderation
  • Dairy: cream and butter are freely allowed; cheese in moderation
Get in the habit of reading labels when grocery shopping. Even products labeled "sugar-free" can contain hidden carbs.
