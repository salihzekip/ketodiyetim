How to Stick With Keto? Breaking Through a Plateau

7 min read · Sustainability · August 12, 2026

The most common issue on the keto diet is the plateau that follows the initial rapid weight loss. When the scale stops moving, many people quit — but this phase is temporary and can be overcome.

Why Do Plateaus Happen?

  • Your body adapts to its new weight, and basal metabolism slows down
  • Water loss stops, making fat loss appear smaller
  • Hidden carb intake may be blocking ketosis
  • The stress hormone cortisol triggers fat storage

5 Strategies to Break a Plateau

  • Calorie cycling: Eat slightly more calories one day a week
  • Tighten your IF window: Move from 16:8 to 18:6
  • Drop carbs to zero: One week of strict keto
  • Add exercise: Muscle mass raises basal metabolism
  • Sleep and stress: 7–8 hours of sleep is key to weight loss
